Boarding policies

Service agreement
A signed service agreement is required before boarding begins.
Meet-and-greet
A meet-and-greet is required before boarding, so the dog can be met and observed in person and prepared for a comfortable stay.
Vaccinations
Dogs must be up to date on rabies, DHPP (or DAPP), and Bordetella vaccinations.
Medication
Medication can be given during a stay. It must arrive in its original container, with a medication log and instruction sheet completed ahead of time.
Pickup and drop-off
By appointment.
Deposit
A deposit of 50% of the boarding total is required to reserve a stay.
Cancellations
Boarding stays require 72 hours' notice to cancel. Major holidays require 10 days' notice. Which days count as major holidays is confirmed when you book.
Emergency and veterinary care
Veterinary information and an emergency veterinary authorization are collected before a stay. If your dog needs emergency veterinary care during a stay, Brandi may arrange it, and you are responsible for the cost of that care.
Bite history and aggression
Please tell Brandi about any bite history or aggression, in full, before a stay. If the history you give is untrue or incomplete, Brandi may end the stay.
Safety
Brandi may decline or end a stay if she believes continuing is unsafe for a dog or a person.
