Dog training and in-home boarding in Colorado Springs

Training for puppies, everyday manners, behavior problems, and aggression and severe reactivity, which are a specialty. Overnight and day boarding are offered as a separate service.

Request a consultation How training works

Aggression and severe reactivity are a specialty

Brandi works with severe aggression, reactivity, bite-history cases, and difficult dogs.

Every case starts with a Consultation & Evaluation, used to understand the dog and build the training plan. It may cover the dog's history, behavior, triggers, and handling, and what you want to be able to do with your dog. When appropriate, it can include interaction with other dogs or people.

What training can address

Brandi starts the work, then hands it to you

The dog learns the system, and the owner learns how to maintain it. Brandi builds a foundation with your dog first, then brings you into the training in stages while your dog is still in the program.

  1. EvaluateMay cover your dog's history, known commands, behavior, triggers, handling, and your goals.Working: Brandi, with your history and goals
  2. EstablishBrandi works with the dog first to build the foundation.Working: Brandi
  3. IntegrateYou are brought into the training while your dog is still in the program.Working: Brandi and you
  4. TransferYou handle your dog more and more while Brandi observes and coaches.Working: you, with Brandi
  5. Support30 days of transition support after your dog returns home.Working: you, at home

Two separate services

Training

An immersive training program of approximately 14 days, with the length adjusted to your dog. Programs run from puppies and foundations through aggression and severe reactivity, and each includes 30 days of transition support.

Training details and prices

In-home boarding

Overnight boarding and day boarding in a home environment. Boarding is its own service, listed and priced apart from training, and does not include structured training.

Boarding details and prices

Serving Colorado Springs, Fountain, Manitou Springs, and the surrounding greater Colorado Springs area.

Brandi Boles

Brandi Boles is the trainer behind Be Still Dog Co. With 10 years of experience working with dogs, she provides individualized training for puppies, everyday manners, behavioral challenges, severe reactivity, and aggression. Her process teaches the dog first, then helps owners learn the commands, tools, structure, and handling needed to continue the work at home.
