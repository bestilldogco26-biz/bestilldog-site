What happens next

For training, the next step is a 60-minute Consultation & Evaluation. The $150 fee is credited toward a training package when you book one. If too much time passes before you book, your dog will need to be reevaluated.

After you send an inquiry, Brandi replies to arrange the next step.

For boarding, the first step is a meet-and-greet.

A signed service agreement is required before training or boarding begins.
