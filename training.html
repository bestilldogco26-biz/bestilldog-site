What training can address

  • Aggression & Severe Reactivity

    A specialty. Cases may include human aggression, dog aggression, severe reactivity, bite history, resource guarding, territorial behavior, fear-related aggression, leash aggression, and other serious behavior concerns. Every case starts with a Consultation & Evaluation.

  • Puppies & FoundationsMay include basic obedience, crate and kennel training, potty routines, household manners, leash manners, recall, place, impulse control, handling, appropriate socialization, and owner instruction.
  • Manners & ObedienceMay include basic and advanced obedience, leash manners, recall, place, household boundaries, impulse control, public manners, customized commands, and owner handling instruction.
  • Behavior ModificationMay include work on reactivity, fear-related behavior, resource guarding, excessive barking, destructive behavior, anxiety-related behavior, poor impulse control, household behavior problems, and other behavior concerns.
  • Specialized / Custom TrainingFor dogs whose needs sit outside a standard category. Examples include changing command words, rebuilding commands that were used inconsistently, creating a new communication system, specialized behavior work, and combinations of obedience and behavior training.

How the work is fitted to your dog

Each program starts with a Consultation & Evaluation, a behavioral evaluation used to understand the dog and build the training plan. It may cover your dog's history, current behavior, temperament, triggers, obedience, handling, the commands it already knows, previous training, reactivity and aggression, and your concerns and goals.

When appropriate, it can include interaction with other dogs or with people, and a look at whether command words or communication methods should change. It may end with an individualized training recommendation.

The immersive program

Brandi works with your dog first to build the foundation. Once it is in place, you are brought into the training while your dog is still in the program, and your part grows until you are handling your dog while Brandi observes and coaches.

Every training package includes 30 days of transition support after your dog returns home. Follow-up is used to answer questions, reinforce handling, and address issues that appear as the training moves into everyday life.

Methods and tools

Training methods are selected for the individual dog and may include food rewards, leash work, structured boundaries, prong-collar training, e-collar training, or other techniques appropriate to the dog and the goals of the program.

When useful, training can also include marker-based reinforcement, changes to existing command words, or a new command system.

Training policies

Service agreement
A signed service agreement is required before training begins.
Vaccinations
Dogs must be up to date on rabies, DHPP (or DAPP), and Bordetella vaccinations.
Deposit and payment
A non-refundable deposit is required to reserve a training package. The deposit is applied to the package price. The deposit amount is confirmed when you book.
Cancellations and rescheduling
Appointments require 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed appointments are charged in full.
Veterinary information and emergency care
Veterinary information is collected before training begins. If your dog needs emergency veterinary care while in Brandi's care, Brandi may arrange it, and you are responsible for the cost of that care.
Bite history and aggression
Please tell Brandi about any bite history or aggression, in full, before training begins. If the history you give is untrue or incomplete, Brandi may end service.
Safety
Brandi may decline or end service if she believes continuing is unsafe for a dog or a person.
Results
Training results depend on the individual dog and on consistent follow-through at home. No specific result is promised.
